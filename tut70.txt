Types of:-
    --Containers
        1)Sequence Containers
            -->Stores data in linear fashion(it shows linearity)
            Ex : Vector, List, Dequeue

            Vector:-
                -->Random Access Fast.
                -->Insertion/deletion slow.
                -->Insertion, at last, is fast.
            List:-
                -->Random Access slow.
                -->Insertion/Deletion at middle & last is fast.
        2)Associative Containers
            -->It is designed for the fastest searchability and deletion.
            -->Direct Access (Map, Multimap, Set, Multiset).
            -->Cannot apply for Random Access.
            -->Random Access is Slow.
        3)Derived Containers
            -->Derived from Sequence and Associative Containers.
            -->Properties Depend on Data Structure.
            -->Mostly made for real-world modelling.
            Ex:- Stack(LIFO Structure), queue(FIFO Structure), priority queue.
